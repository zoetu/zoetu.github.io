Biography

I received my master’s degree in Network and Information Security from the School of Cyber Security, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2024, and my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Hunan Institute of Science and Technology in 2021.

My research interests include model compression and efficient inference, with a focus on mixed-precision quantization, knowledge distillation, layer skipping, and adaptive early exiting for pretrained language models, as well as deployment on edge devices.
